About FILLY

What is FILLY?

FILLY stands for "A Hybrid Filipino Text Normalization and Grammar Correction System for Informal Text Communication Using N-Gram Rule-Based Normalization and GECToR-Based Error Correction."

It helps users improve their Filipino writing by detecting informal, colloquial, and grammatically incorrect words or phrases and suggesting more formal and appropriate alternatives.

Text Normalization

Expands abbreviations (aq→ako, nsa→nasa), decodes leet speak (bah8→bahay), and resolves colloquialisms using N-Gram language models.

Grammar Correction

Applies GECToR sequence tagging to detect and fix grammatical errors using a pre-trained transformer encoder.

Code-Switching Ready

Handles Taglish (Tagalog-English) mixed language texts common in Filipino digital communication.

Hybrid Pipeline

Combines rule-based precision with neural power in a unified two-stage pipeline for comprehensive text improvement.

Welcome to Filly!

Your Filipino Writing Assistant

What is Filly?

Filly helps users improve their Filipino writing by detecting informal, colloquial, and grammatically incorrect words or phrases and suggesting more formal and appropriate alternatives.

Whether you're preparing academic papers, reports, or professional communications, Filly makes your Filipino text cleaner and grammatically correct.

How to Use Filly

1. Enter Your Text

Type or paste your Filipino text into the editor on the left panel.

Filly will analyze your input for informalities, errors, and grammatical issues.

2. Review Recommendations

Suggestions appear in the Recommendations panel on the right.

  • "Accept" the suggested correction
  • "Ignore" the suggestion if you prefer the original

3. Compare Output

The corrected text appears in the center panel. Compare it side-by-side with your original input to see exactly what changed.

Tips for Best Results

Filly provides intelligent suggestions based on its language models and correction rules. Users are encouraged to review all suggested corrections to ensure they match the intended meaning and context.
